# Saved research conversations

Explorer keeps research discussion and the follow-up composer in private local storage, outside the shared journey file. Switching questions or reopening Explorer restores each question's conversation for that revision of its research. Restoring text never starts a model request, restores approval, or grants source access. Both follow-up modes still send the exact reviewed context to the model provider when you explicitly start Research.

The existing drawer contains the conversation, draft save status, and older research selector. Older research and conversations whose saved decision context changed are historical. Once Explorer observes a known context change, that historical boundary is retained across restarts. Research replacement records the boundary before committing the new findings; a failed map save may conservatively leave the discussion historical. They remain readable, but a new request requires a fresh current conversation. Fresh conversation clears the current discussion and preserves its composer; Delete saved conversation removes both for the selected record. Neither changes research findings or your answer.

Each conversation retains at most 20 whole exchanges within 256 KiB. Older whole exchanges are dropped and counted. A request uses at most four complete exchanges within 64 KiB, and may include fewer to fit the total context budget. Exact review identifies the included turns and reports omissions separately from previously dropped turns. An omitted source cannot be used as if it were included.

The private store holds at most 128 conversations within 8 MiB. At capacity, Explorer asks you to explicitly delete saved records instead of silently evicting drafts or other conversations. Management lists expose metadata for cleanup, including records from removed journeys; they do not display another journey's transcript. Metadata listing remains available when a draft or reply cannot save. You can delete unrelated records to free space; a record with an unsaved reply or local draft must be resolved before deleting that record. Generic storage cleanup protects the conversation file.

Drafts save independently from completed discussion. If another canvas changes the same draft, your text stays visible and you choose whether to use the saved draft or keep yours against the latest revision. If a save cannot be confirmed, the interface says so. It does not silently retry or claim the text is durable. Earlier unsaved composers that conflict during a research replacement remain visible under Earlier drafts need attention. You can explicitly save that earlier draft against the latest private record or discard its local copy; neither action changes saved discussion. A validated reply whose save cannot be confirmed remains temporarily available for Save again or Discard; further research waits for that choice. Closing Explorer during a genuine storage failure can lose an unsaved reply. Page-close draft saving is best effort, so check the save status before closing.

Copies and different project-file locations have separate private conversations. Moving a journey does not automatically move its private discussion; old records remain available for explicit metadata-only cleanup. Reopening the same journey at the same location retains its history. Shared exports, imported journey files, native model context and diagnostics omit private transcripts. Direct-file previews without a trusted private storage directory keep conversations only in memory and disclose that limitation.

Version 1 starts empty; Explorer does not infer or import discussions from exported documents. Unknown, malformed or unsafe conversation files are preserved and block writes until repaired. Persisted records contain discussion and composer fields plus bounded freshness fingerprints; they contain no review tokens, model session authority, reuse receipts or hypothetical-use consent.
