# Research model

In **Settings > Research model**, choose **Use selected chat model** (the default) or a dedicated eligible Copilot model, then **Save model**. Opening settings, refreshing the catalog, selecting or saving a choice, and preparing a request review start no inference or retrieval. Research and its follow-ups show their effective model and whether they follow the chat in the pre-request review. The start button is **Research**. The review appears inline in the bottom drawer, with the effective model visible before the expandable exact inputs. Saving a preference refreshes an open ordinary Research review without replacing the answer editor or discussion.

Each review captures the personal preference revision, effective model and private account identity outside the instructions and prompt. Start rechecks fresh host catalog/account metadata and the preference. A changed relevant model, account or preference requires another review. A dedicated choice is independent of later chat-model selections. Once Start accepts the operation, queued and running work keeps that captured model; later changes apply to later requests. The attached chat's model is never changed. Question generation, answer cleanup, question help, ADR helpers and Challenge Review retain their existing routing. The future Research explanation route in #104 must reuse this Research contract.

If a model disappears, becomes disabled or is denied by policy, its saved identifier remains visible. Choose a replacement or explicitly return to the chat default. There is no automatic fallback, account substitution or inference retry. Catalog errors preserve the choice and still permit an explicit reset to the default. A corrupt private settings file is preserved and reported rather than silently reset. Metadata errors do not expose raw SDK messages.

`research-model-settings.json` lives in Explorer's trusted personal data directory alongside existing preferences. It has version 1, a nonnegative revision and `modelId` (null means default). IDs are bounded to 128 characters; reads are limited to 4 KiB and reject duplicate/extra fields, invalid UTF-8 and unsafe links or replaced roots. Writes use the existing owned lock, revision check and atomic replacement. Missing settings mean default without a write. Bare-file previews have no writable preference store. Model/account settings never enter shared maps, native snapshots, history or exports.

`research.mjs` discovers metadata using the installed SDK's `model.list({skipCache:true})`, `model.getCurrent()` and `gitHubAuth.getStatus()`. Metadata discovery has a 20-second deadline. Initial and follow-up browser reviews allow 30 seconds for discovery and local preparation; unrelated reviews retain their 10-second deadline. Expired, cancelled and superseded replies cannot become usable reviews. The eligible catalog projection is bounded and catalog names render as text. A dedicated choice remains usable if the selected-chat metadata is unavailable but its own catalog/account eligibility is valid. The owned helper revalidates the captured model against fresh parent and helper catalogs, matching accounts, isolated workspace, initialized tools and its actual model before inference. Existing deadlines, cancellation, permissions and usage reconciliation remain in force. A model choice grants no additional web permission; #41 and #42 remain unresolved.

After actual helper-model verification, the private `usage/session-state/<id>/operation.json` receipt records `model`. This also works when no usage event includes a model. Usage events keep their original projected model fields and accounting identities; there is no second token ledger. Older receipts may lack the field and remain readable. Previously saved research and conversations are not relabeled with today's preference. Model provenance lives in the private operation receipt, without duplicating it into the shared research schema. Evaluation reports should cite their captured and verified model from this evidence.

Coverage includes private persistence and concurrent writers, catalog/policy/account failures, stale reviews, accepted queued-operation pinning, actual helper-model mismatch, unrelated helper routing, draft/composition events, late UI replies and authorized Brave keyboard/narrow-pane checks. The installed anonymous local-model/MCP fixture verifies captured default and dedicated follow-up models against actual helper models and receipts, without a paid provider or existing session. It does not establish production entitlement, live research quality, physical IME/screen-reader behavior or native Copilot rendering; #91's native acceptance remains pending.
