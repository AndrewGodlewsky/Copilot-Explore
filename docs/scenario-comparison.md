# Compare saved scenarios

Open the library while a journey is active. On a Personal or Project entry, choose **Compare with current**. The dialog names the current saved journey as Left and the selected entry as Right. Choose **Compare these saved journeys** to read their saved content. Comparing does not save answers, drafts, preference, metadata or a new scenario, and does not invoke a model.

The stacked view works with a narrow pane: up to 25 node summaries appear in the selector, followed by one complete selected answer pair. Previous/Next pages cover every saved node in both validated maps (up to 10,000 rows). Abbreviated selector labels are identified; selected question, answer and retained research text remain complete, including Unicode and whitespace. A 1 MiB response limit fails with an explicit source-inspection instruction if a selected comparison cannot fit. Dependency comparison considers every recorded link, but details show the first 25 upstream links per side and their total count. Open the source to inspect additional relationships.

Comparable nodes have unique recorded identity claims in common. Explorer uses the document/root/creation/node identity, portable fork origin and immediate-source mappings, and explicit personal-copy lineage. It never matches by question wording. Conflicting or duplicate aliases stay unpaired and carry an ambiguity warning; imported lineage is a historical claim, not independently verified provenance. Nested forks can therefore be compared without finding or loading their original map. Independently generated questions remain distinct even when their text is identical.

The view distinguishes changed answers and answer states, changed question/context wording, parents, review warnings and causes, research/stale markers, and explicit upstream dependencies. Research basis, sources, retrieval dates, imported provenance, claims and limitations remain available in the selected node's complete recorded research. Absence of a stale marker does not verify freshness. Choosing a comparison or opening a source never promotes an assumption, tentative answer or research recommendation to a decided answer.

**Only Left** and **Only Right** mean observed absence from the other selected map. They do not establish chronological addition or deletion: forks intentionally omit descendants and unrelated branches, and a historical base snapshot is not guaranteed. Shared/different guidance is current recorded context, not a reconstructed starting snapshot. Generation artifacts, reminders, accepted briefs/exports and all private data are outside this comparison. There is no merge, winner selection or preferred-scenario change.

Each comparison lasts at most 15 minutes. Both complete saved sources are checked again before another page or source navigation. Current-map edits, missing or changed files, changed checkout/listing, switching away and back, or page suspension require a new comparison. Project selections retain existing explicit-folder, duplicate, partial-listing and path checks; no additional repository files or historical sources are searched. Project-side comparison revisions are local working values and are not cross-scenario chronological counters.

**Open Left/Right source question** is a separate operation. It flushes private drafts through the usual journey-switch flow and blocks on pending requests, staged questions, unresolved reminder/direction drafts or failed draft persistence. A source changed after review is rejected. Closing the comparison leaves the existing editor mounted; returning from the library keeps its draft and caret. A suspended page discards the dialog and ignores late responses.

Verification covers pure lineage and large-map fixtures, dense ambiguous aliases, exact shared text, evidence/state separation, controller/HTTP read-only behavior, stale/disappeared/expired sources, Personal and Project selections, source navigation, draft recovery, pending work and late UI responses. Native narrow-pane layout, screen-reader presentation and keyboard/focus checks remain pending owner verification. No browser/desktop automation or paid-model calls are used.
